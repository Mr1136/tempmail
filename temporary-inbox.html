What is a temporary inbox?

A temporary inbox is a mailbox intended for short-term email reception. It provides a place to receive messages without making your normal email address part of a temporary online interaction.

Why use a temporary inbox?

Separate temporary mail

Keep short-lived messages separate from important personal email.

Test email delivery

Use a separate inbox when checking whether a website sends email correctly.

Receive one-time messages

A temporary inbox can be useful for short-lived confirmation messages.

Start without signup

Vanish is designed around immediate access rather than traditional accounts.

Temporary inbox vs permanent email

A temporary inbox is best for low-stakes, short-term communication. A permanent email account remains the better choice for important accounts, personal correspondence and anything that requires reliable long-term access.

How Vanish works

  1. 1. Generate: Create a temporary address.
  2. 2. Share: Enter the address where a temporary inbox is needed.
  3. 3. Receive: Incoming messages appear in Vanish.
  4. 4. Delete: Remove the inbox when you are finished.
Temporary inboxes are not intended for passwords, banking, healthcare, account recovery or other information that must be retained permanently.
