What is a temporary inbox?

A temporary inbox is an email inbox intended for short-term use. It can receive messages sent to a temporary email address without requiring you to use your normal personal mailbox.

What can you use it for?

Temporary signups

Receive verification or confirmation messages for short-term registrations.

Development testing

Test registration and email workflows without using personal addresses.

One-off messages

Keep messages from one-time services separate from your normal inbox.

Inbox separation

Reduce unnecessary traffic going to your everyday email account.
