Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. Overview

VanishTemp ("Vanish", "we", "us", or "our") provides a temporary email service that allows visitors to create disposable email addresses without creating an account.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use VanishTemp and how that information is handled.

2. Information We Collect

VanishTemp is designed to minimise the information required to use the service. We do not require users to provide their real name, telephone number, password, or other personal information to create a temporary email address.

When using the service, technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, device information, and standard server logs may be processed by third-party infrastructure providers or advertising providers.

3. Temporary Email Content

VanishTemp uses the public Mail.tm API to provide temporary email functionality. Email addresses and messages displayed through VanishTemp are provided by the Mail.tm service.

VanishTemp does not operate the underlying Mail.tm mail servers and does not claim ownership of email content transmitted through that service.

Temporary email addresses should not be used for confidential, sensitive, financial, medical, legal, or other information that you need to retain.

4. Cookies and Advertising

VanishTemp may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, analytics, security, or advertising.

If advertising is enabled on VanishTemp, third-party advertising providers such as Google may use cookies or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements.

Users can manage advertising preferences through Google's available advertising settings and their browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

VanishTemp relies on third-party services to operate parts of the website. These may include Mail.tm, GitHub Pages, Google services, advertising providers, analytics providers, and content delivery services.

These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Retention

VanishTemp is intended for temporary use. We do not promise that temporary email addresses or messages will remain available for any particular period.

Data handled by third-party services may be retained according to those providers' own policies.

7. Children's Privacy

VanishTemp is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the operation of VanishTemp, please contact us through the Contact page .
