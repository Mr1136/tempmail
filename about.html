About Vanish

Vanish is a temporary email service designed to give you a quick, disposable email address when you don't want to use your primary inbox.

Temporary email addresses can be useful when testing websites, signing up for services, protecting your primary email address, or receiving messages that you don't need permanently.

Our goal

Vanish is designed around simplicity. Generate an address, use it when you need it, and let it disappear when you're finished.

Important limitations

Vanish is intended for legitimate temporary email use. Temporary inboxes should not be used for fraud, phishing, spam, harassment, or other unlawful activity.

Messages received through temporary inboxes may be automatically deleted and should not be treated as permanent storage.
