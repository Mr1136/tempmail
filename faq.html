Frequently Asked Questions

What is temporary email?

Temporary email provides a disposable email address that can be used instead of your primary email address.

Why use a temporary email address?

Temporary email can help reduce unwanted messages to your primary inbox and can be useful for testing websites and receiving one-time messages.

Can I send emails from Vanish?

Vanish is designed primarily for receiving temporary email messages. Sending functionality is not currently provided.

Are temporary emails permanent?

No. Temporary inboxes and their messages are intended to be short-lived. Don't use them to store important information.

Can I recover a deleted inbox?

No. Once an inbox has been deleted or its messages expire, you should assume that they cannot be recovered.

Is Vanish anonymous?

Temporary email can reduce the amount of personal information you provide to websites, but it should not be considered a guarantee of anonymity.
