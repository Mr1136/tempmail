What is 10 minute mail?

"10 minute mail" is commonly used to describe temporary email services that provide an address for a short period. These services are useful when someone needs to receive a message without giving out their normal email address.

Is Vanish exactly 10 minutes?

Not necessarily. Vanish is designed as a temporary email service, but you should not assume that every inbox lasts exactly ten minutes. The service is intended for short-lived use rather than permanent email storage.
