What is a disposable email address?

A disposable email address is designed for temporary or low-priority communication. It gives you an alternative to handing out your personal email address whenever a website requires an email to continue.

Unlike a traditional mailbox that you may keep for years, a disposable inbox is intended for short-lived use. This makes it useful when you don't want temporary communication mixed with important personal mail.
