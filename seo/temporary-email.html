What is temporary email?

A temporary email address is an email address intended for short-term use. Instead of giving a website or service your everyday email address, you can use a disposable address for situations where you only need to receive a message for a limited period of time.

Temporary email can be useful for testing websites, receiving one-time messages, separating low-priority communications from your primary inbox, or avoiding unnecessary marketing mail.

When should you use temporary email?

  • ✓ Testing websites and applications
  • ✓ Receiving one-time confirmation messages
  • ✓ Keeping low-priority mail away from your primary inbox
  • ✓ Signing up for services you only need temporarily
  • ✓ Separating temporary activity from your everyday email

Important: Temporary email should not be used for banking, healthcare, account recovery, legal records, passwords, or any account where permanent access is important.
