What is a temporary email address?

A temporary email address is an email address intended for short-term use. It can be useful when a website asks for an email address but you don't want to use your primary mailbox for a temporary interaction.

How to create a temporary email address

  1. 01
    Open Vanish

    Visit the Vanish email generator.

  2. 02
    Generate an address

    Use the generated temporary email address or choose an available custom address.

  3. 03
    Receive your message

    Messages sent to the address appear in the temporary inbox.

  4. 04
    Delete it when finished

    When the temporary inbox is no longer needed, delete it and move on.

What can a temporary email address be used for?

  • ✓ Testing registration and email workflows
  • ✓ Receiving one-time confirmation messages
  • ✓ Keeping marketing and promotional mail away from your main inbox
  • ✓ Temporary online services and low-stakes communication
Don't use temporary email for important accounts. Banking, healthcare, account recovery and other services that require long-term access should use a permanent email address.
