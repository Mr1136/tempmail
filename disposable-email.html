What is a disposable email?

A disposable email address is an email address intended for short-term use. Instead of giving a website, service or online form your normal email address, you can use a separate address for receiving temporary messages.

This can help keep your primary inbox separate from newsletters, confirmation messages, testing emails and other communications that you may not want associated with your everyday inbox.

Why use disposable email?

Reduce inbox clutter

Keep temporary registrations and one-off messages away from your main inbox.

Avoid unnecessary signup

Create an address without creating another permanent email account.

Useful for testing

Test registrations, forms and email workflows without using your personal inbox.

Short-term by design

When you are finished, you don't need to keep a permanent mailbox for the address.

Don't use temporary email for important accounts

Temporary email is best suited to short-term and low-importance communications. For banking, healthcare, account recovery and other important services, use an email address you control permanently.
