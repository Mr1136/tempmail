What is 10 minute mail?

"10 minute mail" generally refers to a temporary email service designed for short-lived use. The idea is simple: create an address, receive the message you need and avoid maintaining another permanent inbox.

When should you use a permanent email?

Temporary email is not appropriate for every situation. If you need to recover an account later, receive important notifications or maintain a long-term relationship with a service, use a permanent email address that you control.
