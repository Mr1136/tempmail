Why use temporary email when testing a website?

Website developers and testers often need to check what happens when a new user registers. That can involve confirmation emails, welcome messages, password-reset flows and other automated messages. A temporary inbox provides a separate destination for those tests.

What can you test?

Registration emails

Check that registration messages arrive after a new signup.

Email verification

Test whether verification messages reach a real inbox.

Welcome emails

Review automated messages generated after registration.

Email delivery

Confirm that your application sends email to the expected destination.

Simple website testing workflow

  1. 1. Create an inbox.

    Generate a temporary Vanish email address.

  2. 2. Enter the address.

    Use it in the registration or email form you're testing.

  3. 3. Trigger the email.

    Submit the form or perform the action that should generate an email.

  4. 4. Inspect the result.

    Check whether the expected message arrives in the temporary inbox.

When a temporary inbox isn't enough

For serious automated testing, development teams may need dedicated test mail infrastructure, staging environments or tools specifically designed for automated email capture. Vanish is best suited to quick manual checks and low-complexity testing.
