How Vanish Works

1. Generate an email address

Vanish creates a temporary email address for you. You can use the randomly generated address or create an available custom address.

2. Use the address

Enter your Vanish address when you need an email address but don't want to provide your primary inbox.

3. Receive your message

Messages sent to your temporary address appear in the Vanish inbox automatically.

4. The inbox is temporary

Temporary email is not intended to replace a permanent email account. Messages can expire and should not be used as long-term storage.
