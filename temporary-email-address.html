What is a temporary email address?

A temporary email address is an email address intended to be used for a limited period or a specific purpose. It provides an alternative to handing over your everyday email address whenever a website asks for an email.

Vanish is designed to make that process simple: generate an address, use it to receive the message you need, and avoid adding another permanent mailbox to manage.

Common uses

Temporary registrations

Receive a confirmation message without putting your main inbox on another mailing list.

Website testing

Test signup and verification flows without repeatedly using a personal address.

One-off messages

Keep short-lived communications separate from your normal inbox.
